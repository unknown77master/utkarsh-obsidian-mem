# Policy Violation Linkage

## User

Close linkage with a network of accounts or other entities that violate or evade our policies
means?

## Assistant

It means there is a strong connection (for example, shared ownership, control, coordination, or use of the same resources) between an account and other accounts or entities that have broken rules or are trying to circumvent enforcement actions such as suspensions or bans. In other words, even if an account itself has not yet violated a policy, its close association with policy-evading accounts may itself be considered problematic under that policy.
