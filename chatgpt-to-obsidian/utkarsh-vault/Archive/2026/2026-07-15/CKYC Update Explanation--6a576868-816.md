# CKYC Update Explanation

## User

Dear Customer, your CKYC number is currently not available in our records. As per RBI guidelines, we will update it by downloading it through CERSAI. If you wish to opt out, SMS CERSAINO to...... within 7 days from the date of this communication - Axis Bank

What does this mean

## Assistant

This is a **legitimate informational message** that many banks send to comply with RBI's Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements.

Here's what it means:

- **CKYC (Central KYC)** is a centralized KYC system managed by **CERSAI (Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India)**.
- Axis Bank is saying that **your CKYC number is not currently linked to your account** in its records.
- To update its records, the bank plans to **download your CKYC information from the CERSAI database** if it exists.
- This helps avoid asking you to submit KYC documents again in the future.

The message also says:
- If you **do not want Axis Bank to retrieve your CKYC data from CERSAI**, you can opt out by sending the specified SMS (e.g., "CERSAINO") within 7 days.

### Should you do anything?
- **If you're comfortable with the bank updating your KYC records**, you don't need to do anything. The bank will proceed automatically.
- **If you don't want the bank to access your CKYC record**, send the opt-out SMS as instructed.

This message **does not mean there's a problem with your bank account**. It's simply informing you about a KYC records update.

If you're unsure whether the SMS is genuine, verify it by:
- Checking whether it came from an official Axis Bank sender ID.
- Calling Axis Bank customer care using the number on the back of your debit/credit card or visiting your nearest branch. Never call numbers mentioned in suspicious SMS messages.
