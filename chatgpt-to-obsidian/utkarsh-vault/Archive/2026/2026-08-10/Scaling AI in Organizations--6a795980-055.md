**AI is easy to demo. Building AI that an organization can actually scale is the hard part.**

I recently attended **Community Connect – Scaling AI in Organizations**, organized by **Indian Data Club**, and walked away with a perspective that goes beyond models, prompts, and benchmarks.

In **Toni Ramchandani’s session**, I had to change my perspective completely.

Instead of thinking like an engineer building an AI solution, I had to think like a **founder or decision-maker**:

> *If I am responsible for this company, would I actually deploy this?*

That question changes everything.

A promising AI prototype is only the beginning. To create real business value, you need to think about the **workflow, data, adoption, ownership, risk, monitoring, cost, and measurable outcomes**.

One idea that particularly stood out to me was starting with the **workflow, not the model**.

Before asking *“Which AI model should we use?”*, the better questions are:

**What problem are we solving? 
What is the baseline? 
Where does AI genuinely add value? 
What happens when it fails? 
And who owns the outcome?**

That is a very different way of looking at AI startups.
