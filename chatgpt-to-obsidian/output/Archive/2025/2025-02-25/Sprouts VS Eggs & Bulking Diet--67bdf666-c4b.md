### **Can You Eat Raw Eggs?** 
Yes, but it's **not recommended** due to the risk of **Salmonella infection** and poor protein absorption. 

### **What is Salmonella Enteritidis?** 
Salmonella Enteritidis is a **bacteria** that can be present in raw eggs. It can cause **food poisoning**, leading to: 
- **Fever** 
- **Diarrhea** 
- **Vomiting** 
- **Stomach cramps** 

### **Why Cook Eggs Instead?** 
1️⃣ **Kills Salmonella** – Cooking eggs at **70°C+** destroys bacteria. 
2️⃣ **Better Protein Absorption** – Your body absorbs **90% of cooked egg protein** but only **50% from raw eggs**. 
3️⃣ **No Biotin Deficiency** – Raw eggs contain **avidin**, which blocks biotin (vitamin B7) absorption. Cooking deactivates avidin. 

### **Safer Alternatives to Raw Eggs** 
- **Pasteurized Eggs** (heat-treated to kill bacteria) 
- **Cooked Eggs** (boiled, scrambled, omelet) 
- **Egg Whites Only** (raw whites have less Salmonella risk but still lower absorption) 

For **bulking**, eating **cooked eggs** is always the better choice! Want recipe ideas? 🚀
