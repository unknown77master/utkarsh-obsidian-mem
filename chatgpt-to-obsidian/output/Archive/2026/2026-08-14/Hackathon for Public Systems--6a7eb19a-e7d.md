### 2. What is EPFO?

**EPFO = Employees' Provident Fund Organisation.**

It is an organization under India's **Ministry of Labour and Employment** that manages the **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)** system.

Think of EPF as a **retirement savings system for eligible employees**:

- A portion of an employee's salary is contributed toward their provident fund.
- The employer also contributes.
- The money accumulates as savings for the employee.
- EPFO maintains these accounts and provides related services.

For example, EPFO's online services are used for things like:
- Checking your PF balance
- Viewing your contribution/passbook
- Transferring PF when changing jobs
- Updating certain account details
- Initiating PF withdrawal/claims
- Managing your **UAN (Universal Account Number)**

So when the post jokes:

> “EPFO login captcha alone has probably killed more startup productivity…”

They're basically referring to the **frustrating user experience of government EPFO online services**, particularly things like login, CAPTCHA, session timeouts, and website usability.

The hackathon is essentially asking: **“Can engineers build a significantly better experience around these kinds of public services?”**
