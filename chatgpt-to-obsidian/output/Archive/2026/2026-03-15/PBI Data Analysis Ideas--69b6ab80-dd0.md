# PBI Data Analysis Ideas

## User

give me ideas of data analysis PBI dashboard or project

## Assistant

Here are **good Power BI (PBI) Data Analysis project ideas** you can build. These are suitable for an **engineering student building a portfolio for Data Analyst roles**, because they demonstrate **data cleaning, modeling, visualization, and insight generation**.

---

## 1. E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard
**Objective:** Analyze sales performance of an online store.

**Dataset:** Amazon / Flipkart / Kaggle E-commerce dataset 

**Key Analysis:**
- Total revenue and profit
- Top selling products
- Sales by region and category
- Monthly sales trend
- Customer segmentation

**Power BI Visuals:**
- KPI Cards (Revenue, Profit, Orders)
- Line chart for monthly sales
- Map for regional sales
- Bar chart for top products

**Insights Example:**
- Identify highest revenue category
- Find low performing regions
- Seasonal demand patterns

---

## 2. Netflix Content Analysis Dashboard
**Objective:** Analyze Netflix shows and movies dataset.

**Dataset:** Netflix dataset (Kaggle)

**Key Analysis:**
- Movies vs TV Shows distribution
- Content added per year
- Top genres
- Country-wise content production
- Duration analysis

**Visualizations:**
- Pie chart (Movies vs Shows)
- Bar chart (Top genres)
- Timeline (Content added over years)
- Map (Country production)

**Insights Example:**
- Which country produces most Netflix content
- Which genre dominates platform

---

## 3. Global COVID-19 Data Analysis
**Objective:** Study pandemic trends across countries.

**Dataset:** WHO / Our World in Data

**Key Analysis:**
- Total cases and deaths
- Country comparison
- Vaccination trends
- Mortality rate

**Visualizations:**
- World map
- Line chart for cases over time
- KPI cards
- Country comparison bar chart

**Insights Example:**
- Countries with highest vaccination rate
- Mortality rate differences

---

**Visualizations:**
- Scatter plot
- Bar charts
- KPI cards
- Distribution histogram

**Insights Example:**
- Relationship between study hours and marks
- Subject with lowest performance
