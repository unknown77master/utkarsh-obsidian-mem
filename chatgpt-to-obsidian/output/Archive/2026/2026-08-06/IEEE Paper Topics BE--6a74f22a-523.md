# IEEE Paper Topics BE

## User

i want to publish research paper for my BE degree, suggest me some topics, and i am intrested in publishing paper in ieee

## Assistant

Publishing an IEEE paper as a BE student is a great idea, especially if you plan to pursue AI, Data Science, or Software Engineering roles. The key is to choose a topic that is:
- Novel enough to contribute something new.
- Practical enough to complete within 4–8 months.
- Supported by publicly available datasets.
- Easy to demonstrate with experiments.

Here are some strong topic ideas, ranked by their potential.

## 1. Agentic AI for Data Analysis (Highly Recommended)

**Title**
> Multi-Agent AI System for Automated Data Analysis and Business Insights

**Idea**
Develop multiple AI agents that:
- Clean data
- Perform exploratory data analysis
- Generate visualizations
- Recommend ML models
- Produce a business report

**Technologies**
- LangGraph
- CrewAI
- AutoGen
- Python
- Pandas
- LLMs

**Research contribution**
Compare multi-agent systems with a single-agent approach.

# If you want an IEEE paper with a higher chance of acceptance

Avoid overly common topics like:
- Iris classification
- Titanic prediction
- Basic sentiment analysis
- Simple fake news detection
- Stock prediction with LSTM

Instead, add a research contribution such as:
- A new algorithm or workflow.
- A comparison of multiple state-of-the-art methods.
- An optimization that improves accuracy, speed, or cost.
- A hybrid architecture (e.g., RAG + reranking, CNN + Transformer).
