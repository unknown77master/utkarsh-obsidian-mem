## Project 1A: Quantitative Risk Dashboard 

This project focuses on designing an interactive dashboard to monitor and analyze financial risk metrics. The dashboard integrates multiple data sources such as market prices, portfolios, and historical returns. Key risk indicators like Value at Risk (VaR), Expected Shortfall, volatility, and correlation are visualized in real time.

The objective is to help decision-makers quickly identify risk exposure, trends, and abnormal movements. This project enhances skills in data visualization, risk modeling, and business intelligence tools, enabling effective communication of complex quantitative insights.

## Project 1B: Liquidity Crisis 

This project simulates a liquidity crisis scenario in financial institutions or markets. It studies how sudden withdrawal of funds, market panic, or asset illiquidity affects financial stability.

The project involves analyzing cash flow mismatches, funding gaps, stress testing, and scenario analysis. The main aim is to evaluate the institution’s ability to meet short-term obligations and to design strategies for liquidity risk management. This project builds understanding of real-world financial crises and regulatory risk controls.

---

## Project 1C: Causal Inference Trading 

This project applies causal inference techniques to trading strategies. Instead of relying only on correlation, it identifies cause-and-effect relationships between economic variables and asset prices.

Methods such as propensity score matching, regression discontinuity, and instrumental variables are used to validate whether a factor truly impacts price movement. The objective is to design more reliable trading strategies with reduced false signals. This project strengthens analytical thinking and improves model robustness in quantitative trading.

---

## Project 1D: Gamma Reactor Management 

This project deals with managing gamma exposure in options trading. Gamma measures how fast delta changes with respect to price movement of the underlying asset.

The project focuses on hedging strategies to control risk during high volatility. It involves portfolio rebalancing, sensitivity analysis, and stress testing. The goal is to minimize losses caused by rapid price fluctuations. This project enhances understanding of derivatives, risk sensitivity, and advanced portfolio management.
