# Professional Invitation Request

## User

how to say , please accept my invitation request of professional email

## Assistant

If you're referring to a LinkedIn or professional networking invitation, here are a few polished ways to say it:

- **Please accept my connection request.** ✅ (Most natural for LinkedIn)
- **I would appreciate it if you could accept my connection request.**
- **I hope you'll consider accepting my connection request.**
- **Please accept my invitation to connect.**
- **I would be grateful if you could accept my invitation to connect.**
- **I look forward to connecting with you. Please accept my connection request if you have a moment.**

If you're writing an email, you could end it with:

> Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to connecting with you. Please accept my connection request at your convenience.

Using **"connection request"** is more professional and common than **"invitation request."**
