# Meeting Time Calculation

## Assistant

**Solution (as written in an examination answer style):**

Given, 
Length of circular track \(= 400 \, m\) 

Speed of first runner \(= 5 \, m/s\) 
Speed of second runner \(= 3 \, m/s\)

Time taken by first runner to complete one round:

\\[
t_1 = \frac{400}{5} = 80 \, s
\\]

Time taken by second runner to complete one round:

\\[
t_2 = \frac{400}{3} \approx 133.33 \, s
\\]

They will meet again at the starting point when both complete an integer number of rounds together.

Let us check common time:

In \(400 \, s\):

First runner completes:
\\[
\frac{400}{80} = 5 \text{ rounds}
\\]

Second runner completes:
\\[
\frac{400}{400/3} = 3 \text{ rounds}
\\]

Both reach the starting point together after \(400 \, s\).

---

**Answer:** 
\\[
\boxed{400 \text{ seconds}}
\\]

(Correct option: **400 sec**)
