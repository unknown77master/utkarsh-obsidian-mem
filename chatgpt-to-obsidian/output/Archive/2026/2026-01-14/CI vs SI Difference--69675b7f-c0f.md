# CI vs SI Difference

## User

Difference between CI & SI on 1000 at 10% For 2 years

## Assistant

We can solve this **step by step** to find the difference between **Compound Interest (CI)** and **Simple Interest (SI)**. 

**Given:** 
- Principal \(P = 1000\) 
- Rate \(R = 10\%\) per annum 
- Time \(T = 2\) years 

---

### Step 1: Calculate Simple Interest (SI)

\\[
SI = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}
\\]

\\[
SI = \frac{1000 \times 10 \times 2}{100} = 200
\\]

---

### Step 2: Calculate Compound Interest (CI)

For **annual compounding**, 

\\[
CI = P \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T - P
\\]

\\[
CI = 1000 \times \left(1 + \frac{10}{100}\right)^2 - 1000
\\]

\\[
CI = 1000 \times (1.1)^2 - 1000
\\]

\\[
(1.1)^2 = 1.21
\\]

\\[
CI = 1000 \times 1.21 - 1000 = 1210 - 1000 = 210
\\]

---

### Step 3: Find the Difference

\\[
CI - SI = 210 - 200 = 10
\\]

---

✅ **Answer:** Difference = **10**

## User

A:B is 3:2 what is fraction of A

## Assistant

If the ratio of \(A : B = 3 : 2\), the **fraction of A** in the total is calculated as: 

\\[
\text{Fraction of A} = \frac{A}{A + B} = \frac{3}{3 + 2} = \frac{3}{5}
\\]

✅ **Answer:** \(\frac{3}{5}\)

## User

Average of first 10 natural no.

## Assistant

The **average of the first \(n\) natural numbers** is given by: 

\\[
\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of first } n \text{ numbers}}{n}
\\]

The **sum of first \(n\) natural numbers** is: 

\\[
S_n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
\\]

For the **first 10 natural numbers** (\(n = 10\)):

\\[
S_{10} = \frac{10 \times (10+1)}{2} = \frac{10 \times 11}{2} = 55
\\]

\\[
\text{Average} = \frac{55}{10} = 5.5
\\]

✅ **Answer:** 5.5
