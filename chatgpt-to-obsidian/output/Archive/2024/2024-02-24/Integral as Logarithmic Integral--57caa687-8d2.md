# Integral as Logarithmic Integral

## User

Integration of 1/xlogx

## Assistant

The integral ∫(1/x * log(x)) dx can be expressed in terms of special functions. It is denoted as Li(x), where Li is the logarithmic integral function. So, the result is Li(x) + C, where C is the constant of integration.

## User

What is the answer to Integration of 1/xlogx

## Assistant

The integral of \( \frac{1}{x \log(x)} \) doesn't have a simple closed-form expression in terms of elementary functions. It is commonly denoted as \( \text{li}(x) \), the logarithmic integral function. So, the answer is \( \text{li}(x) + C \), where \( C \) is the constant of integration.
