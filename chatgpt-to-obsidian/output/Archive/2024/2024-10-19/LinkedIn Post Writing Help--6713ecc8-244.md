## Assistant

LinkedIn doesn't support clickable links directly within post text. However, you can add URLs as plain text, and they will be clickable when published. Here's how you can include links effectively:

1. **Add URLs at the end of the post**: This is the most common method. Simply paste the full URL, and LinkedIn will make it clickable.

2. **Use the comments**: You can write "Link in the comments" in your post, then paste the link in the first comment.

3. **Shorten URLs**: Use URL shorteners like Bitly to make long links look cleaner.

Example:
```
Check out more about the event here: \[URL\]
```

For longer posts, keeping the URL at the end can make the post look cleaner and more professional.

## Assistant

To tag people in your LinkedIn post, you can use the "@" symbol followed by the person's name. LinkedIn will show a list of people as you type, and you can select the correct profile to tag them.

Here’s how you can do it in your post:

1. Start typing the name of the person you want to mention, like `@Omkar Bondge`.
2. LinkedIn will give you suggestions. Select the correct person from the list.
3. Once tagged, their name will appear as a clickable link in your post.

Example for your post:

---

🎉 Thrilled to share that my team and I secured **first place** in the **IBM Qiskit Quantum Computing Hackathon**! 🚀

Special thanks to my amazing teammates, **@Omkar Bondge** and **@Yuvraj Gaykhe**—couldn’t have done it without your hard work and collaboration! 🙌

---

This way, their names will link directly to their LinkedIn profiles.
