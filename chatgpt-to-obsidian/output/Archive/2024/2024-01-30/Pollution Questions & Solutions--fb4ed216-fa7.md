2. Water pollution impacts aquatic ecosystems and human health by introducing harmful substances such as chemicals, heavy metals, and pathogens into water bodies, disrupting ecosystems and posing health risks.

9. Technological innovations can reduce pollution by promoting clean energy sources, improving waste management, and developing eco-friendly alternatives to traditional products.

10. Policies and international collaborations are crucial to address air pollution globally. Measures include emission regulations, sustainable development goals, and coordinated efforts to combat climate change.
